Before you capture

  1. Wash hands before taking the image.
  2. Capture individual nails clearly.
  3. Avoid taking pictures directly under a tubelight or with flash to minimize reflections.
  4. Avoid applying nail paint, mehndi, or other products before image capture.
  5. Do not use fake/artificial nails.
